Feel Meter Support

Feel Meter: Drum Timing · Privacy Policy

Feel Meter listens to your drums through your iPhone or iPad and shows, stroke by stroke, whether you rush or drag against the click.

Need help? Email synactllc@gmail.com. Tell us your device, whether you play a kit or a pad, and what you see on screen. A screenshot helps a lot.

Where to put your phone

Acoustic kit

On its own stand or a chair, never on the kit. On the kit, the mic hears the stand more than the drums.

Practice pad or desk

On the same table as the pad, about a hand's width from where you tap. The table carries your strokes to the mic. A separate table can work in a quiet room.

Common problems

Strokes on mesh heads are not counted

Mesh heads and electronic kits are too quiet for the mic. Play on a rubber pad, a desk or an acoustic kit.

Click only, no timing

Bluetooth and AirPlay play the click too late to measure. To see your timing, use the phone speaker or plug in wired headphones.

"Could not hear the click"

On the phone speaker, turn the volume up, then tap Start again.

Strokes are missed

  • Check your case leaves the mic holes on the bottom edge open. Still missing strokes? Take the case off.
  • On a pad, put the phone on the same table, a hand's width from where you tap, and put the pad on something hard.

Clicks count as hits after you stop

Turn the volume down a little, or move the phone a bit farther from the pad.

Fan or room noise

Keep the phone away from the fan. The app adjusts to the room by itself.

Microphone is off

Open Settings, tap Privacy & Security, tap Microphone, then turn on Feel Meter.

The app has the same tips: tap Troubleshooting on the Home screen.

Protect your hearing

Drums and headphones are loud. Keep the click at a comfortable level and wear hearing protection when you play.
